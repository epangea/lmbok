# Academy — sessions

The Academy is where the actual learning happens. Every session an AI generates for you moves through five phases:

1. **Warmup** — a light entry point into the topic
2. **Explore** — the core content
3. **Challenge** — something to actively try or answer
4. **Reflect** — a moment to connect it back to your own life
5. **Assess** — a check that adapts to how you're doing, paired with a Socratic companion rather than a pass/fail grade

Sessions are generated fresh each time, shaped by your [Prosopon](prosopon) — your interests, your phase of life, your practice so far — rather than pulled from a fixed course.
