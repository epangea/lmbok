# Avatar stages

Your avatar grows through six stages as your practice deepens — based on experience points (XP) and how broadly you've explored across skills and across the three groups of the [15 Arts](15-arts) (Being, Becoming, Connecting):

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 🫘 Seed | Where everyone starts |
| 🌱 Sprout | 18+ XP, a couple of skills touched |
| 🌿 Sapling | 50+ XP, 4+ skills touched |
| 🌳 Grove | 100+ XP, 6+ skills, breadth across at least 2 of the 3 art groups |
| 🌲 Forest | 200+ XP, 9+ skills, breadth across all 3 groups |
| 🌍 Ecosystem | 500+ XP, 12+ skills, full breadth across all 3 groups |

This is deliberately about breadth as well as volume — going deep in one narrow area alone won't move you up as far as genuinely exploring across Being, Becoming, and Connecting.

Your avatar stage also determines how much of [Polis](polis) is open to you.
