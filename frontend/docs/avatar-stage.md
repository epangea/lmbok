# Avatar stages

Your avatar grows through six stages as your practice deepens — based on your **weakest** [Art](15-arts), not your strongest. Every one of the 15 Arts has to reach a stage's score threshold before you advance — going deep in just a few won't move you up if the rest are untouched.

| Stage | What it takes |
|---|---|
| 🫘 Seed | Where everyone starts |
| 🌱 Sprout | All 15 Arts at 10%+ |
| 🌿 Sapling | All 15 Arts at 20%+ |
| 🌳 Grove | All 15 Arts at 40%+ |
| 🌲 Forest | All 15 Arts at 65%+ |
| 🌍 Ecosystem | All 15 Arts at 100% |

This is deliberately about roundedness, not volume in any one favorite Art — your weakest Art is always the bottleneck.

Your avatar stage also determines how much of [Polis](polis) is open to you.
