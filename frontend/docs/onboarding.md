# Onboarding — what happens when you join

When you first join, the platform gets to know you in four short steps:

1. **Language** — the language you'll see the platform in
2. **Your name** — how you'll appear on your journey
3. **Your phase of life** — expecting a child, infant, child, adolescent, adult, or elder — so the platform meets you where you actually are
4. **A few things worth knowing about you** — three short, optional, voice-friendly questions that seed your [Prosopon](prosopon) from day one

Your avatar's look (its symbol and color) is chosen randomly rather than picked by you — one less decision standing between you and your first session. You can change how you appear later from your profile menu.

Every question here is skippable. The platform will simply learn more about you as you go instead.
