# Ekklesia — organizations

Ekklesia is where organizations and learners meet. Organizations list real needs — tasks, skills, or qualifications they're looking for — and learners are matched to them by bioregion, interests, and ability level.

Matching goes through a consent step: candidate lists are anonymized until both sides agree to a match, at which point identities are revealed. Once matched, an org rep can verify real-world completion of tasks and skill demonstrations directly against a learner's sessions and LECKOs — a citable, low-friction alternative to a traditional interview.
