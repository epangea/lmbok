# Bioregion

Your Bioregion page connects you to the place you live — not by political borders, but by the living, geographic region around you: its watershed, climate, species, and the people who describe it.

Learners contribute portraits of their own bioregion in their own words; the platform can help draft a starting point from your location, which you're free to accept, edit, or ignore. Once a bioregion has a portrait nearby, you can read it instead of starting from scratch.

Your bioregion also feeds into [Polis](polis), the platform's civic-dialogue space, once you've reached the avatar stage that unlocks it.
