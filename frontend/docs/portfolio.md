# Portfolio, your progress

The Portfolio is your record of growth over time. At the top you see your avatar stage, how many sessions you have done, your hours, and how close you are to the next stage. Below that are four tabs:

- **Story**, your Guiding Star and My Story, the profile the platform builds from your sessions and companion conversations. Once My Story has changed, you can also see how it read earlier, and you can talk it over with the companion
- **Growth**, how your three families (Being, Becoming, Connecting) and your weakest Art have moved over time, with each avatar stage marked, and your progress in each of the 15 Arts
- **Journey**, a timeline of the sessions you have completed and the milestones along the way, such as your first steps in an Art, a skill reaching a new level, or your avatar growing into a new stage. Your weekly stories appear in the timeline too
- **Skills**, the skills behind each Art, so you can see where to practice next

If an organization has verified any of your skill demonstrations through [Ekklesia](ekklesia), those citable, org-vouched credentials show in the Skills tab, kept separate from your own self-driven progress, so it's always clear which is which.
