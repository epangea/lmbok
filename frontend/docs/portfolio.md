# Portfolio — your progress

The Portfolio is your record of growth over time, in three tabs:

- **XP chart** — your experience points over time
- **Session history** — every session you've completed
- **Per-art skill breakdown** — how your progress spreads across the 15 Arts

If an organization has verified any of your skill demonstrations through [Ekklesia](ekklesia), those citable, org-vouched credentials show here too — kept separate from your own self-driven progress, so it's always clear which is which.
