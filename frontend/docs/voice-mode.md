# Voice mode

Voice mode lets you listen to prompts and answer by speaking instead of typing — useful on a phone, while walking, or if reading isn't your preferred way to take in a session.

It uses your browser's own built-in speech features (no audio is ever sent to a server): a speaker icon reads content aloud, and a microphone button lets you dictate into any answer field, live, with a visible transcript as you speak.

Turn it on from the toggle near the top of a session. It works across sessions and, where offered, on other text fields too — including the [onboarding](onboarding) questions.
