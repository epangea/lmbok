# Mouseion — skills map

The Mouseion is where you can see your skill progress laid out visually, grouped into 8 practical domains (48 skills total, 6 per domain):

Cognitive & Intellectual · Creative & Artistic · Physical & Motor · Social & Relational · Language & Communication · Emotional & Psychological · Meta-Learning · Tools & Systems

This 8-domain grouping exists as a familiar, plain-language entry point — a bridge for learners who haven't yet gotten comfortable with the platform's deeper organizing structure, the [15 Arts](15-arts). Under the hood, the same skills also map onto the 15 Arts; the Mouseion view is just a different lens on the same progress.

Click any skill in the Mouseion to jump straight into a session focused on it.
