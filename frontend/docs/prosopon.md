# Prosopon — your learner profile

**Prosopon** (from the Greek *πρόσωπον*, "face" or "person") is what we call the platform's evolving picture of you as a learner.

It isn't a form you fill out once. It's a living record that grows every time you use the platform — what you've said, what you've practiced, what you've reflected on, where you are in life. The platform reads it before generating your sessions, so your sessions can actually be *for you* rather than generic.

## What goes into it

- **The 3 questions from onboarding** — what you'd ask, what you care about, what you want to do. These are the very first thing your Prosopon knows about you.
- **Sandbox conversations** — anything you talk through with the platform's reflective companion gets distilled (by AI, briefly) into your profile: curiosities, recurring themes, how you communicate.
- **Your finished sessions** — when you complete a session, what you wrote in its challenge and reflection is distilled into your profile too.
- **Alongside it** — your phase of life, your bioregion and the skills you've practiced also shape your sessions directly.

## What it's used for

Every time a new session is generated for you, your Prosopon is part of what shapes it — the examples used, the tone, the kind of challenge offered. The generator is also shown short excerpts of what you wrote in your recent sessions, and the titles of your latest sessions on other Arts, so a thread that surfaced somewhere else can carry over. It is told to use all this as background and not to quote it back to you — and to stretch you where an Art calls for it, not only confirm what you already love. The idea is simple: **everything the platform learns about you should shape your sessions, and every session should, in turn, add to what it knows.**

Sessions served from the shared library (for example, if the AI is unavailable) were not written for you, so they are not shaped by your Prosopon.

## Where it goes

To write a session for you, the text above is sent to the AI service that generates it. The [privacy note](/privacy.html) describes this.

## Your control over it

Nothing here is used to judge, grade, or rank you. You can skip the onboarding questions entirely — the platform just starts with less to go on, and fills in the picture as you go instead.
