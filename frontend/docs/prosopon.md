# Prosopon — your learner profile

**Prosopon** (from the Greek *πρόσωπον*, "face" or "person") is what we call the platform's evolving picture of you as a learner.

It isn't a form you fill out once. It's a living record that grows every time you use the platform — what you've said, what you've practiced, what you've reflected on, where you are in life. The platform reads it before generating your sessions, so your sessions can actually be *for you* rather than generic.

## What goes into it

- **The 3 questions from onboarding** — what you'd ask, what you care about, what you want to do. These are the very first thing your Prosopon knows about you.
- **Sandbox conversations** — anything you talk through with the platform's reflective companion gets distilled (by AI, briefly) into your profile: curiosities, recurring themes, how you communicate.
- **Your practice** — skills you've built evidence for through sessions, your phase of life, your bioregion, your avatar.

## What it's used for

Every time a new session is generated for you, your Prosopon is part of what shapes it — the examples used, the tone, the kind of challenge offered. The idea is simple: **everything the platform learns about you should shape your sessions, and every session should, in turn, add to what it knows.**

## Your control over it

Nothing here is used to judge, grade, or rank you. You can skip the onboarding questions entirely — the platform just starts with less to go on, and fills in the picture as you go instead.
