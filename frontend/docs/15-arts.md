# The 15 Arts

Everything on this platform is organized around 15 Arts of being human, grouped into three families of five:

**Being** — Move · Eat · Feel · Notice · Express
*Inner-connectedness, bodily wisdom, emotional awareness, creativity.*

**Becoming** — Live · Listen · Give · Receive · Collaborate
*Personal rights and duties, empathy, compassion, shared action.*

**Connecting** — Understand · Respect · Build · Grow · Consume
*First principles, respect for all life, regenerative building and stewardship.*

You don't need to know this taxonomy to get started — onboarding no longer asks you to pick a "first art" up front, since it's not a fair ask before you've experienced any of them. It's here for when you're curious about the deeper structure, or want to browse deliberately by group instead of following the platform's recommendations.

The [Mouseion](mouseion)'s 8-domain skill grouping is a friendlier, more everyday-language view onto this same underlying set of skills.
