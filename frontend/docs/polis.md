# Polis — civic dialogue

Polis is the platform's space for civic deliberation — structured dialogue about real proposals affecting your community or bioregion.

Access widens as your [avatar stage](avatar-stage) grows: early stages see local, community-level discussion; higher stages unlock regional and eventually global-scope dialogue. The idea is that a track record of genuine learning and practice, not an account age or a payment, is what earns a bigger seat at the table.
